# Reference Models — HIOL Vol. 28

Both PDFs live in `10_Context/Reference_Models/`. They are the empirical
base of [[HIOL_Style_Guide]].

## [[HIOL_28_06_mcneill_gibson.pdf|McNeill Gibson — "Between the Poet and the Clown"]]
- pp. 125–139 · ~20 sources · no endnotes
- **Key takeaways:** contextual hook → case study by page two; theory
  (Peacock, Candido, Yúdice) introduced *while analyzing*; Portuguese terms
  italicized + glossed (*pedagoginga*, *malandragem*); full translations
  after quoted Portuguese; thematic headings (*Persona Poética/Poeta Palhaço*);
  conclusion returns to the social stakes via Candido on literature as
  citizenship.

## [[HIOL_28_01_alessandri_carlos.pdf|Alessandri Carlos — "The Poiesis of the City and of Resistance"]]
- pp. 32–46 · ~12 sources · 2 endnotes · **translated by Karina Mosling**
  (the translation-credit precedent for our project)
- **Key takeaways:** opens with an epigraph (Buzo); explicit hypothesis
  paragraph; Lefebvre/Ribeiro woven into spatial analysis; thematic headings
  (*The Space of Poiesis in the Periphery of São Paulo*); closing section
  "Bringing It Together" restates the hypothesis and gestures outward to
  the possible-impossible.

> [!tip] Thematic overlap with our project
> Both models treat marginal spaces and bodies as sites of *poiesis* and
> resistance — the same register our article needs when handling the
> *pichicera* and the nocturnal plaza.
