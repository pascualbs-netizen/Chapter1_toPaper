# Target Venue: *Hispanic Issues On Line* (HIOL)

## Confirmed Facts
- **Type:** Refereed, open-access scholarly electronic series, founded 2006,
  devoted to Hispanic and Lusophone cultures.
- **Host:** University of Minnesota Digital Conservancy
  (`cla.umn.edu/hispanic-issues/online-volumes`).
- **Model:** Themed volumes and "Debates" — submissions are usually tied to
  a specific **Call for Papers**, not rolling.
- **Style:** MLA 9th Edition (field standard; confirmed in both reference
  models — see [[Reference_Models_Index]]).
- **Length:** ~6,000–8,000 words including notes and Works Cited
  (both reference models run 15 printed pages).
- **Translation precedent:** Alessandri Carlos's article in Vol. 28 is
  explicitly "Translated by Karina Mosling" — translated scholarship is
  accepted and credited.
