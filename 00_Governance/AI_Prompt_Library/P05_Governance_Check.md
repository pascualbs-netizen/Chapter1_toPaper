# P05 · Governance Check

**Use when:** End of each sitting, before closing the vault.
**Paste first:** [[Context_Bundle_Paste_First]].
**Then paste:** everything written or revised that session.
**File output:** the checklist result at the top of that day's log, or
appended to the session note in `30_Archive/`.

```
Run a governance check on today's work against the binding rules:
1. Word budget — current section total vs. budget; projected article total
   vs. 7,500 (ceiling 8,000).
2. Dissertation-ese — quote any surviving hedging, meta-commentary, or
   front-loaded theory, with a cut suggestion.
3. Theory anchoring — flag any paragraph of theory without a primary-text
   anchor.
4. Term protocol — any Spanish term missing italics or first-use gloss.
5. Consistency — does today's prose contradict the thesis as stated in
   §I or the Vault Constitution?
Return: PASS/FAIL per item + one-line fixes. End with the single most
important action for the next session.

TODAY'S WORK:
[paste]
```
