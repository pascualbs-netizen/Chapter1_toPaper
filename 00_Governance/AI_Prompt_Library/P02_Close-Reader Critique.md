# P02 · Close-Reader Critique

**Use when:** A drafted passage feels theoretically loose or stylistically off.
**Paste first:** [[Context_Bundle_Paste_First]].
**Then paste:** the passage + the Spanish original it draws on (if any).
**File output:** apply accepted suggestions to [[Article_Master_Draft]];
log rejected ones under a dated heading in `30_Archive/Critique_Log.md`.

```
Critique this passage on three levels. Do not rewrite it; tell me how to
fix it.
1. THEORETICAL PRECISION — Am I applying Wynter/Weheliye/Quijano/Agamben
   correctly, or stretching their concepts? Is the Agamben-critique-via-
   coloniality move intact?
2. TEXTUAL GROUNDING — Does every claim touch the primary text? Where is
   the close reading thin, and which Spanish passage should I return to?
3. HIOL REGISTER — Present tense? Confident claims? Any dissertation-ese?
   Any sentence an Anglophone reader without Argentine/Chilean context
   would miss?
Then give 3 concrete, prioritized revision moves.

PASSAGE:
[paste]
```
